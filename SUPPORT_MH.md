# SwapVision Support — Meta Quest

SwapVision is a tile-swapping picture puzzle game for Meta Quest, published by Meshy.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

- **The goal:** a picture is cut into square pieces and scrambled. Swap the pieces until the picture is whole. The finished picture is shown beside the board.
- **Swap:** point at a piece and select it (controller trigger, or pinch with your hand), then select another: the two trade places. Select the first piece again to cancel.
- **Turn:** in later worlds each piece has several faces, and only one is right. Point at a piece, hold the trigger (or pinch) and drag across it to turn it: up, down, left or right. Silver and Gold have coins with two faces, Platinum and Emerald pieces with four faces, Diamond to Legend cubes with six.
- A level is done when every piece is in the right place showing the right face. A piece in the right place with a wrong face flashes red after each move.
- **No timer, no move limit:** SwapVision only counts your moves (each swap and each turn is one). Every solved level earns three stars and 10 points.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb shows the solution: every piece moves home and turns to its right face. The first time on a level it uses one solution credit; showing it again is free. Watching the solution does not solve the level: the level starts over after a moment, for you to solve.
- A short guided tutorial runs the first time you open the game; **How to Play → Replay Tutorial** runs it again.
- Controllers and hands both work. Controller B or Y (Back) closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend, built on 100 pictures, from 4 pieces up to 36.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, levels open in order: the next level opens when you solve the one before it.

## Purchases

- If a purchase was interrupted, SwapVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**.
- Solution credits that you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in SwapVision for Apple devices stay there. Nothing transfers between Apple devices and Meta Quest.
- For a refund, follow Meta's refund policy in the Meta Horizon Store.

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your headset. If cloud backup is on in your headset's settings, Meta Horizon OS backs it up, and it comes back when you install SwapVision again.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
