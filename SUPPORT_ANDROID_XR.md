# SwapVision Support — Android XR

SwapVision is a tile-swapping picture puzzle game for Android XR devices, published by Meshy on Google Play.
Contact: **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** — we answer within 48 hours.

## Playing

- **The goal:** a picture is cut into square pieces and scrambled. Swap the pieces until the picture is whole. The finished picture is shown beside the board.
SwapVision opens as a window in your space. Point at a piece with your hand (or look at it, or use a controller) and pinch.

- **Swap:** pinch a piece, then pinch another: the two trade places. Pinch the first piece again to cancel.
- **Turn:** in later worlds each piece has several faces, and only one is right. Pinch a piece and drag across it to turn it: up, down, left or right. Silver and Gold have coins with two faces, Platinum and Emerald pieces with four faces, Diamond to Legend cubes with six.
- A level is done when every piece is in the right place showing the right face. A piece in the right place with a wrong face flashes red after each move.
- **No timer, no move limit:** SwapVision only counts your moves (each swap and each turn is one). Every solved level earns three stars and 10 points.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb shows the solution: every piece moves home and turns to its right face. The first time on a level it uses one solution credit; showing it again is free. Watching the solution does not solve the level: the level starts over after a moment, for you to solve.
- A short guided tutorial runs the first time you open the game; **How to Play → Replay Tutorial** runs it again.
- **Back** closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend, built on 100 pictures, from 4 pieces up to 36.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, levels open in order: the next level opens when you solve the one before it.

## Purchases

- Purchases go through Google Play, with your Google account.
- If a purchase was interrupted, SwapVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Google account that made the purchase.
- Worlds come back on any device signed in to that account. Solution credits are part of your saved game: credits you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in SwapVision on other platforms stay there. Nothing transfers between platforms.
- For a refund, see Google Play's refund policy: [support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your device. If backup is on for your Google account, Android backs it up, and it comes back when you install SwapVision again; without backup, uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
